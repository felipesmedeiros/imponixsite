Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

PriceTracker helps you track and compare the prices of groceries and other goods. This policy applies to PriceTracker, provided by Imponix Game Studio.

PriceTracker data

PriceTracker does not add personal tracking or build personal advertising profiles. We use the product, store, price, and receipt information you choose to provide to offer price comparisons. Reviewed price information may be shared in the community catalog.

If you use account, receipt scanning, location, or notification features, information needed for those features is processed to provide the service. Receipt processing may use OpenAI services. Please avoid including personal or payment details in receipt images. We do not send your receipts, shopping lists, email address, or app-provided location in ad requests.

Google AdMob

PriceTracker uses Google AdMob when ads are enabled, including test ads during development. The app requests non-personalized ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect IP addresses, device identifiers such as advertising IDs, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Non-personalized ads can still involve this processing. Rewarded ads may use a random session reference to verify a reward.

Where required, the app asks for advertising consent. You can review available choices through Ad privacy choices in your profile and manage advertising identifiers in your device settings.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

You can manage optional permissions in your device settings and request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. We keep service data as needed to operate PriceTracker, verify contributions, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Some contribution or audit records may remain after deletion. We do not sell your personal information.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or concerns about a child's information, email contact@imponix.com and mention PriceTracker. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
