# Version 1.2.0 is almost here

This weekend, we plan to update the Game Store Chronicle pre-release build with nearly everything intended for version 1.2.0. If the final tests go as expected, we intend to bring the update to the official build next week.

## This weekend's pre-release

- The pre-release build will receive **most of the version 1.2.0 changes** during the weekend of August 15–16.
- This will be our last broad testing pass before the official release. We will be watching for stability issues, unexpected save behavior, and anything that needs final balancing or polish.
- Because this will still be a pre-release build, a few details may change before the update reaches everyone.

## A city that feels alive

- The old regular-customer system will be replaced by a persistent population of **293 residents**. People will keep their identity and appearance within each save and will be able to return as pedestrians or potential customers.
- Pedestrians will move through dedicated sidewalk and crosswalk routes, walk at personal speeds, give one another space, and stop for short conversations around the city.
- Store visits will begin with a real decision at the storefront. Rating, stock, capacity, time, personality, window displays, the greeter, active boosts, and progression will all be able to influence who walks inside.
- Once inside, customers will observe actual shelves, consider products through their interests, compare prices, avoid products they already own, and make multiple shopping decisions.

## A deeper store economy

- The new optional **Simulation Mode** will add store rent, a managed backroom lease, electricity bills, and paid repairs while keeping wages, product orders, furniture, decoration, and expansions part of every game.
- Players who prefer a simpler experience will be able to keep **Simulation Mode turned off**. With it off, the game will use the lighter economy without store rent, backroom rent, electricity bills, or paid repairs, while regular costs such as wages, product orders, furniture, decoration, and expansions will still apply.
- Store expansions will affect daily rent, powered lights and display booths will affect the electricity bill, and repair work will become another operating cost to manage.
- Five new boost cards—including VIP Magnet, Bigger Baskets, Store Shield, Power Saver, and Rent Relief—will create new ways to shape each run.
- Paid Fast Delivery, generation-aware billboards, cardboard merchandise figures, and the new Generation 3 checkout counter will add more decisions and personality to the shop floor.

## Better tools for every kind of store owner

- **Three save slots** will arrive with individual previews, playtime, cash, progression, game mode, Simulation Mode, and mod information, plus Steam Cloud support for all three slots.
- **Sandbox Mode** will add tools for changing the date, money, store level, rating, deliveries, and NPC population so you will be able to experiment with the store on your terms.
- Store Reports will be rebuilt with clearer finances, customer funnels, lost sales, margins, inventory, utilities, employee output, promotions, customer feedback, and boost-card results.
- GSC Mod Studio will be updated for the three-slot save layout and for local and Steam Workshop mod sources.

## Performance, controls, and polish

- NPC pooling, distance-based update tiers, better crowd scheduling, and loading-time visual warm-up will reduce gameplay spikes while keeping the city active.
- Saving work will be moved away from active gameplay where possible, checkout autosaves will be lighter, and expensive menu data will be built incrementally.
- Steam Deck will receive a dedicated renderer and memory profile, alongside broader gamepad navigation, prompt, focus, and scrolling improvements.
- Version 1.2.0 will also add **Spanish language support**, expanded graphics settings, improved day-and-night lighting, storefront reflections, and a long list of interface and stability fixes.

## What happens next

- We will use the pre-release weekend to validate this large set of simulation, save, performance, and presentation changes together.
- If the build behaves as expected, **our plan is to release version 1.2.0 on the official branch next week**. We will share the complete patch notes with the official release.
- Thank you to everyone testing, reporting issues, sharing feedback, and helping us make Game Store Chronicle better. This is a big update for our two-person studio, and we are excited to finally put it in your hands.
